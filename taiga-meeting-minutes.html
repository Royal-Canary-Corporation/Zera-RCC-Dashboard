Secret Gang × Taiga
Visual Identity

Date: 10 June 2026  ·  Prepared by: Zera  ·  Source: Брифинг — Александр Степанов

Key Outcome

Three visual identity concepts were presented for the Taiga banya product. The team is aligning toward Concept 3 as the primary direction — reinforced with Russian cultural motifs drawn from Concept 1 — targeting the modern international consumer through sensory-driven design with unmistakable Russian identity.

Strategic Direction: Concept 3 + Concept 1 Integration

Ground the brand in the modern luxury sensory language of Concept 3 — textures, tactility, natural scale — then layer in unmistakable Russian cultural cues from Concept 1, so that international audiences feel both the grandeur of nature and the depth of Russian identity simultaneously.
LayerWhat to IntegrateHow
Cultural Identity Slavic ornamental motifs from Concept 1 Embed as subtle surface patterns on textured materials; use as secondary graphic system, not dominant
Color Red accent from Concept 1 Introduce as deliberate punctuation within Concept 3's natural palette (wood, moss, charcoal)
Typography Elongated letterforms from Concept 1 Apply to headlines and logo mark; reinforces taiga scale within Concept 3's minimalist framework
Photography Natural, unposed style from Concept 1 Align Concept 3's macro texture shots with authentic, unstaged Russian banya environment
Graphic System Slavic symbols as micro-details Use on embossed/relief elements, packaging edges, stamp marks — tactile Russian identity

Brand Guardrails

✓ Do

  • Modern, premium sensory experience
  • Russian motifs as refined detail
  • Nature at grand scale (taiga, forest, sky)
  • Tactile materials that feel distinctly Russian
  • Minimalist structure with cultural depth

✕ Avoid

  • Folkloric, rustic, or archaic aesthetics
  • Russian motifs as dominant/literal decoration
  • Generic "spa" or neutral wellness aesthetics
  • European or Grecian visual drift
  • Overcrowding with pattern or ornament
