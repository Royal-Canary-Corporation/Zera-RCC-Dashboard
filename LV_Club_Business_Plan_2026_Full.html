Tax Impact on Revenue

Combined VAT + SCT = 45% of Gross Revenue goes directly to government taxes. On $29.4M Gross Revenue, ~$13.2M is paid in VAT+SCT, leaving Net Revenue of ~$16.2M. This is why GGR margin optimization and player quality (higher-ADT, lower promotional cost) is critical to bottom-line improvement.

Section 04
Marketing & Player Acquisition
Objectives
Objective 01

Position La Victoire as the premium eGaming destination for high-net-worth foreigners in HCMC — the most prestigious club in District 1.

Objective 02

Build a loyal, high-ADT player base across Korean, Chinese, Japanese, and inbound segments — reducing single-player concentration risk.

Objective 03

Leverage the Le Méridien / Marriott Bonvoy ecosystem for cross-promotional opportunities — hotel-to-casino conversion and loyalty integration.

Objective 04

Establish dedicated BD functions per market segment (Korea, China, Japan, Middle East, SEA) — structured outreach and relationship management.

Objective 05

Close the FY2026 budget gap — accelerate CoinIn from $2.07M/day (YTD) to $3.05M/day required in H2 through player quality and visit frequency improvement.

Objective 06

Create and maintain a luxury gaming experience mirroring Macau and Singapore's finest clubs — brand perception, service excellence, ambiance.

Marketing Strategy — 4 Pillars
Pillar 01
Branding & Premium Positioning
  • Luxury brand identity mirroring Macau/Singapore top-tier clubs
  • Interior and experiential design reflecting exclusivity and sophistication
  • Digital presence showcasing VIP services and exclusive events
  • Indirect marketing approach (gaming regulations compliance)
Pillar 02
VIP Guest Experience & Service Excellence
  • Personal host program for Diamond and Platinum tier players
  • Tailored rewards and anticipatory service delivery
  • Korean/Chinese/Japanese-speaking host team
  • Premium facilities, lounge access, and F&B integration
Pillar 03
Hotel & Ecosystem Cross-Promotions
  • Marriott Bonvoy loyalty integration — points earning at LV
  • Hotel concierge activation — target 8%+ hotel-to-club conversion
  • Minimum 250 room nights/month tied to player program
  • F&B cross-redemption — gaming points redeemable at hotel restaurants
Pillar 04
Dedicated BD Teams by Market Segment
  • Korea BD: dedicated manager, community outreach, KakaoTalk channels
  • China/Taiwan BD: WeChat community, F&B partnership, travel agents
  • Middle East: Marriott GCC network, Ahmad Omair signal activation
  • Inbound: MICE partnerships, travel agencies, OTA hotel bundles

12-Point Action Plan
Action 01
Hotel Conversion Program

Concierge incentive scheme — LV daily welcome offer for hotel guests. Target: conversion rate 2% → 8% within 6 months. Minimum 250 room nights/month tied to gaming visits.

Action 02
VIP Host Program

Assign dedicated hosts to all Diamond/Platinum players. Personal contact on visit days. Birthday/event recognition. Host-to-player ratio: 1:8 for Diamond, 1:15 for Platinum.

Action 03
Member Referral Initiative

Existing VIP introduces qualified new player → host incentive + tier upgrade points. Target: 5 qualified new players per month via referral within 90 days of launch.

Action 04
Tiered Rewards Program

Launch formal tier system (Diamond/Platinum/Gold/Silver) with quarterly reviews. Benefits span gaming, hotel amenities, F&B, events, and travel packages.

Action 05
Events Calendar

Quarterly VIP tournaments, monthly themed events, seasonal special nights. Bi-monthly jackpot tournament series. New player introduction vehicle — 20+ attendees per event.

Action 06
Expat Community BD

Hire dedicated BD Manager for Korean + Chinese expat segments. KakaoTalk/WeChat private club communities. District 7 + Binh Thanh Korean business association outreach.

Action 07
Digital & Mobile Platform

Mobile app for bookings, promotions, and rewards tracking. Digital ads via indirect channels (gaming regulation compliant). WeChat/KakaoTalk communities for invite-only engagement.

Action 08
Travel Agent Program

Commission-based partnerships with Korean/Chinese/Singaporean travel agents routing high-spend clients to Le Méridien + LV. Target: 10 active agency partnerships by Q3.

Action 09
MICE & Corporate Partnerships

Partner with MICE event organizers, luxury brand activations, and corporate event venues. Position LV as preferred post-event entertainment destination for international delegates.

Action 10
Machine Floor Optimization

Fix Fortune King Delux (0.4% hold → target 7%). Replace BFC Bats Dragon (-8.9% hold). Scale up 5 Dragons Delux (+2 units). Review Baccarat configuration for hold improvement.

Action 11
Player Lifecycle Automation

CRM-driven reactivation for lapsed players (45+ days). Personalized outreach with tailored incentive. Target: 30% reactivation rate. Automated monthly player statements and tier progress updates.

Action 12
Analytics & Performance Monitoring

Weekly KPI dashboard review. Monthly player segment analysis. Quarterly hold rate review by game. Real-time daily performance monitoring vs budget. Forecast vs actuals reporting.

Strategic Summary

La Victoire Club is positioned at the intersection of two powerful growth vectors — HCMC's booming international tourism market and Le Méridien Saigon's premium hospitality infrastructure. The path to $23.5M GGR in FY2027 requires focused execution across three priorities: retain and deepen existing high-value players (Korean segment), activate the hotel-to-casino conversion opportunity (currently underutilized at ~2%), and fix the floor inefficiencies (Fortune King Delux, Baccarat hold rate) that are costing $300K+ annually. The marketing investment of $880K/yr against an incremental GGR target of $6.5M+ represents a 7x+ return — one of the highest ROI marketing opportunities available to RCC.
