Memorandum of Understanding

Khu Biệt Thự

Branded Residence Advisory — Tuần Châu, Hạ Long

Royal Canary Corporation × One Heritage
Project 18 Luxury Villas · Ha Long Bay
Brands Under Review Roberto Cavalli Home · Visionnaire × Longevity Suite
Date July 2026
— Recitals
WHEREAS I

Royal Canary Corporation ("RCC") is developing Khu Biệt thự Lan Ngọc — an 18-unit luxury villa project comprising four typologies (Sea Villa Type 1 & 2; Marina Villa Type 1 & 2) within the Tuần Châu International Tourism & Entertainment Master Plan, Hạ Long City, Quảng Ninh.

WHEREAS II

RCC seeks to engage an internationally recognised branded concept — combining Italian luxury design identity with a differentiated wellness/longevity lifestyle proposition — to maximise asset value, elevate buyer profile, and position the project within the global premium villa market.

WHEREAS III

One Heritage (Công ty CP Một Di Sản) has demonstrated a track record in operator selection, brand licensing, and luxury project advisory in Vietnam (Gran Melia Nha Trang, New World, Rixos, Sofitel Diamond Crown Hai Phong), and possesses active relationships with Italian luxury design brands including Roberto Cavalli Home and Visionnaire.

WHEREAS IV

Two brand pathways have been identified as strategically aligned with the project vision: (a) Roberto Cavalli Home — bold Italian fashion-forward branded residence identity; (b) Visionnaire × The Longevity Suite — integrated Italian luxury design combined with science-backed longevity and wellness programming, representing a pioneering new residential category (announced January 2026; expanded May 2026 with Luca Dini Design & Architecture).

NOW THEREFORE

The parties agree to collaborate under the following non-binding terms to advance a detailed proposal and preliminary commercial terms for one or both brand options, with the objective of proceeding to a formal Advisory Services Agreement.

01 Purpose

1.1

The purpose of this MOU is to engage One Heritage to provide branded residence advisory services for the Tuần Châu project, specifically:

Option A — Fashion Luxury

Roberto Cavalli Home

Bold Italian fashion-branded residence identity. License held by Oniro Group (Italy); DAMAC Group/Marquee Brands strategic partnership announced May 2026. Active projects: Dubai Canal Residences, Cyprus (Leptos Group). Positioning: ultra-luxury, high-fashion aesthetic, international HNW buyer appeal.

Italian Fashion Bold Luxury Dubai · Cyprus Precedent HNW International

Option B — Longevity Living

Visionnaire × Longevity Suite

Italian luxury interior architecture (60+ years; bespoke environments) × The Longevity Suite (biohacking & anti-ageing clinic network) × Luca Dini Design & Architecture. Partnership announced January 2026; full concept unveiled May 2026. Typologies include: Longevity Villas & Private Residences, Longevity Compounds, Longevity Resorts & Hotels. Positioning: premium wellness/longevity lifestyle, science + design convergence, aspirational health-forward buyer.

Italian Design Wellness · Longevity Science-Backed Villa · Resort Typology Luca Dini Architecture

Why Visionnaire × Longevity Suite is a priority option

The Visionnaire × Longevity Suite concept is purpose-built for villa and resort typologies — directly mirroring the Tuần Châu project profile. CEO Luigi Caterino has stated: "those built under the banner of luxury names in fashion, design and automotive industries can't compare with the Visionnaire × Longevity Suite concept." With Ha Long Bay's natural wellness setting, this combination creates a globally unique narrative: Vietnam's first Longevity-branded luxury villa enclave.

1.2

This MOU is non-binding and constitutes an expression of intent to proceed toward a formal Advisory Services Agreement. It does not obligate either party financially until a formal contract is executed.

02 Scope of Work

One Heritage shall deliver the following across three phases:

A

Brand Intelligence & Fit Assessment

Within 30 days of formal contract signing

A1 Roberto Cavalli Home — Brand Profile Ownership structure (Oniro Group license; DAMAC/Marquee Brands partnership), licensing model, representative projects (Dubai Canal, Cyprus), indicative fee benchmarks
A2 Visionnaire × Longevity Suite — Brand Profile Full concept overview (Visionnaire + The Longevity Suite + Luca Dini Design & Architecture); licensing/partnership model; villa & resort typology specification; wellness programming integration (digital health monitoring, in-residence clinic, bespoke FF&E); precedent projects; indicative fee benchmarks
A3 Comparative Brand Matrix Fit score vs. Tuần Châu across: location context (Ha Long Bay), villa typology alignment, Vietnam buyer profile, international buyer appeal, price premium potential, brand exclusivity in Vietnam, operational complexity
A4 Initial License Fee Estimate Indicative range for: licensing fee, design fee, FF&E premium, fitout premium per unit — both brands. Comparison against Vietnam market benchmarks
B

Formal Brand Outreach & Letter of Intent

Brand outreach initiated Day 1; responses within 45 days

B1 Formal Outreach Letters One Heritage to send introduction letters to: (i) Roberto Cavalli Home via Oniro Group / Marquee Brands; (ii) Visionnaire and The Longevity Suite — on behalf of RCC, positioning Tuần Châu as Vietnam's inaugural project for each brand
B2 Project Information Package Tailored project brief for brand submission: Ha Long Bay setting, project vision, 18-villa scale, RCC profile, target buyer profile, Vietnam luxury market context
B3 Brand Response Minimum one (1) confirmation or expression-of-interest letter from each brand, or written explanation from One Heritage if a brand declines to engage
B4 Preliminary Commercial Terms Indicative term sheet from any responding brand(s): licensing structure, fee ranges, design obligations, exclusivity scope
C

Recommendation Report & Negotiation Strategy

Within 15 days following Phase B completion (approx. Day 60)

C1 Final Comparative Report Brand recommendation (single brand or dual-brand option), financial impact modelling (revenue premium, ASP uplift, construction cost delta), risk factors, Vietnam market timing assessment
C2 Negotiation Strategy Suggested opening positions on licensing fee, design obligations, exclusivity radius, and success metrics for full licensing negotiation
C3 Go / No-Go Recommendation One Heritage's professional recommendation to RCC with clear rationale; proposed next steps for advancing to formal licensing agreement

05 Representations & Expectations

5.1

One Heritage represents that it has, or can establish, working relationships with the brand holders and licensing representatives of Roberto Cavalli Home (via Oniro Group / Marquee Brands) and Visionnaire × The Longevity Suite, and will pursue outreach through appropriate professional channels with well-prepared materials.

5.2

RCC acknowledges that brand responses are subject to the respective brand holders' discretion. One Heritage cannot guarantee positive responses but commits to professional, targeted outreach that positions the Tuần Châu project in the most compelling manner.

5.3

Both parties agree that this MOU engagement is exploratory and advisory in nature. The execution of any formal brand licensing agreement shall be a separate, subsequent commercial process between RCC and the relevant brand holder.

5.4 — Exclusivity

During the term of this engagement and for six (6) months following its conclusion, One Heritage shall not actively propose Roberto Cavalli Home or the Visionnaire × Longevity Suite combination to any competing developer for a branded residence project located in Quảng Ninh Province.

06 Confidentiality

6.1

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this MOU and all project-related information, financial details, and strategic intentions strictly confidential.

6.2

One Heritage may disclose project details to brand representatives solely for the purpose of advancing outreach under this engagement, and shall obtain appropriate non-disclosure undertakings from brand contacts where reasonably practicable.

07 General Terms

7.1

This MOU shall remain in effect for 90 days from the date of signing, or until a formal Advisory Services Agreement is executed, whichever is earlier.

7.2

Either party may terminate this MOU with 7 days' written notice prior to formal contract execution, without liability to the other party.

7.3

This MOU does not create any employment, agency, partnership, or joint venture relationship between the parties.

7.4

Governing Law: The Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Disputes shall be resolved first by good-faith negotiation; if unresolved within 30 days, by the competent Vietnamese court having jurisdiction.

7.5

This MOU is prepared in English. A Vietnamese translation may be prepared for internal use; in case of discrepancy in any subsequent formal contract, the Vietnamese version shall prevail for legal purposes.
