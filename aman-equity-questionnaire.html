💡 Framing Philosophy — "Unknown Unknowns"

RCC operates in hospitality at a strong mid-to-upper-luxury tier. Aman operates in a different category entirely — one where conventional hospitality logic often inverts. Occupancy is not maximized. Price is not the primary signal. Marketing is nearly invisible. Guests are curated, not acquired.

This questionnaire is designed around the assumption that RCC does not yet know the full shape of what we don't know. The questions span three modes: Self-education (what RCC must learn independently), Ask Aman (what only Aman can answer), and Request Aman to educate us (structured knowledge transfer sessions we should request). Every question is tagged accordingly.

Unknown #1
The Aman Guest is not a hotel customer
Aman's guest has typically visited 5–15+ Aman properties. They do not "discover" Aman — they are already initiated. Acquiring them requires zero traditional marketing and everything relationship-based.
Unknown #2
50% occupancy at Aman = success
At $1,500–$3,000+/night ADR, 50% occupancy often outperforms a 5-star competitor at 85% occupancy in total revenue per available room. Aman never fills to capacity by design.
Unknown #3
The brand IS the moat — and it's fragile
A single bad guest experience — especially by a new owner unfamiliar with the culture — can damage decades of brand equity. Aman investors must understand: you own equity, not the guest relationship.
