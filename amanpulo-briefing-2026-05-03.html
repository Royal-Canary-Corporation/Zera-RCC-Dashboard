Part II — Questions & Investment Return Logic (IRL)

🔍 Ownership & Legal Questions

1. How is the 60-40 rule structured in SSRL's current cap table?
Is there a nominee arrangement? Can Aman Group's 20% be considered "Filipino-controlled" for purposes of the foreign equity cap?
2. Who owns Pamalican Island — SSRL or the Soriano family directly?
Land lease vs. freehold? What are lease terms, renewal conditions, and assignability to a new investor?
3. What is Aman Group's proposed post-buyout cap table?
Aman Group target stake? Will they maintain operator role under new structure? Any tag-along / drag-along provisions?
4. Are there any existing pledges, mortgages, or encumbrances on the SSRL shares or island?
Clean title confirmation required in VDR.
5. Has Aman Group signed LOI or binding agreement with Soriano/Aboitiz for the buyout?
What exclusivity period exists? Is RCC entering as co-buyer or secondary investor post-Aman buyout?

💰 Financial & Valuation Questions

6. What is the ask price for the 80% stake (Soriano + Aboitiz combined)?
Implied EV? Has independent valuation been commissioned? Who is the financial advisor?
7. What is the full capex requirement for renovation — scope, timeline, phasing?
Will resort close during renovation? Estimated revenue loss during closure? Who funds interim OpEx?
8. Provide last 5 years audited financials: Revenue, EBITDA, Net Profit, Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR.
Why is occupancy trending down 3 consecutive years (59.5% → 53% → 46%)? Structural or weather-driven?
9. What is the post-renovation revenue / EBITDA projection?
Comparable: After Six Senses Laamu renovation, RevPAR increased ~40%. What is Aman's modeled uplift for Amanpulo?
10. What are the dividend policy and distribution mechanics for shareholders?
Historical dividend history from SSRL? Preferred return structure for new investor?

🏨 Operational Questions

11. What does the Aman management agreement look like — fees, term, termination?
Base fee (typically 2-3% revenue) + incentive fee (10-12% GOP). Duration and break clauses?
12. What is the renovation scope — full rebuild or targeted refurbishment?
Will Aman add new keys? Any branded residences being added (Aman's recent strategy)?
13. What is the aircraft/charter arrangement — owned or outsourced?
What is the annual cost? Asset or OpEx? Will this be sold/restructured?
14. Current forward booking and waitlist status post-renovation announcement?
Any cancellation risk? How is demand holding up given occupancy decline?
