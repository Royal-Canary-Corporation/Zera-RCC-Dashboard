Gaming Operations Strategy

Core
Objective

Maximize gaming efficiency, productivity, and profitability while maintaining a strong player experience.

  • Optimize machine performance and floor productivity through real-time data tools for quick decisions on floor management.
  • Maintain balanced hold rate vs. player satisfaction via regular review processes that keep the right equilibrium between engagement and house advantage.
  • Continuously refine game mix, denomination strategy, and floor layout using performance data and player behavior insights.
Key Focus Areas
CPUPD-driven machine optimization Underperforming machine replacement Strategic jackpot design Machine setting calibration Hold rate alignment
↗

Gaming operations will be managed as a dynamic, performance-driven system—not a static floor—driven by continuous monitoring, reliable data, and a consistently strong player experience.

2

Human Resource Strategy

People
Objective

Build a high-performing, consistent, and scalable workforce capable of supporting operational excellence, leadership development, and future expansion.

  • Develop internal talent pipeline — retain strong internal talent to ensure leadership continuity.
  • Institutionalize T&D, SOPs, and control frameworks for consistent team performance.
  • Embed consistent service culture — define and reinforce standards that deliver professional, responsive, and personalized player experience across all touchpoints.
Key Focus Areas
Structured onboarding & training Clear SOPs Control frameworks Service excellence Leadership alignment
↗

The organization will be structured around standardized processes and clear accountability, ensuring consistent performance and scalability as the business expands.

3

Market Position & Expansion Strategy

Growth
Objective

Strengthen LV Club's position as a leading eGaming destination in Vietnam by building a strong, loyal, and high-value player base. Growth will come from quality rather than volume.

Target Segments

High-value players, expat business owners in HCM & surrounding provinces, and inbound foreign casino players leveraging the integrated hotel-gaming offering at Le Méridien Saigon.

VIP Development

Structured acquisition, retention, and reinvestment frameworks to maximize player value, ensure service excellence, and support long-term profitability through dedicated VIP management.

Business Development

Build dedicated BD functions to identify new markets, develop strategic partnerships, and drive acquisition of high-value player segments across expat and inbound channels.

Entertainment Expansion

Expand entertainment offerings with VIP lounges, KTV, and complementary experiences through internal resources and strategic third-party operators to enhance engagement and total spend.

KPIs to Track
Revenue per nationality Local Expat vs Inbound mix Revenue per room occupancy Gaming vs non-gaming conversion
↗

Market leadership is achieved through consistency and credibility, not short-term visibility.

Products & Services Ecosystem

Experience
Objective

Enhance total player value and revenue contribution by leveraging an integrated ecosystem across hospitality, dining, entertainment, and travel.

  • Integrate hotel, F&B, hospitality, and entertainment to deliver a seamless end-to-end customer experience and maximize total spend.
  • Position LV as a lifestyle destination — combining gaming with premium hospitality, dining, and entertainment for a differentiated proposition.
Key Focus Areas
Hotel-to-casino conversion Integrated loyalty & cross-redemption Curated F&B offerings VIP personalized journey Seamless travel-to-gaming experience
↗

We grow revenue by delivering a smooth and enjoyable player experience across the whole journey, not just on the gaming floor.

Technology & Innovation Strategy

Tech
Objective

Leverage technology to enhance operational efficiency, strengthen control, and elevate the player experience while enabling scalable growth.

  • Implement automation & analytics — deploy integrated digital platforms including mobile apps and CRM to streamline operations and enable data-driven decision-making.
  • Enhance speed, accuracy, and decision-making by integrating technology across key functions to reduce manual processes and improve data reliability.
Key Focus Areas
Cashless systems Player & performance analytics Automated reporting AI for insights & risk detection CRM & personalization
↗

Technology enables scale without increasing complexity.

6

Execution Agility & Decision-Making

Operations
Objective

Improve speed and effectiveness of decision-making to ensure timely response to operational and market changes.

  • Reduce unnecessary complexity in approval processes — shift from over-analysis to controlled execution.
  • Empower management to act within defined parameters with clear decision authority levels.
  • Introduce a test-and-adjust approach — pilot changes instead of prolonged analysis, with defined decision timelines.
↗

In a gaming environment, speed of execution directly impacts revenue. The ability to act quickly—while maintaining control—will be a key competitive advantage.

Conclusion

At the end of the day, the focus is simple—improve performance, build the right player base, and make better use of what we already have. Growth will come from focusing on the right players, building long-term relationships, and delivering a consistent and reliable experience.

We will execute this in phases, adjust based on results, and make sure the business keeps improving over time. The priority is not just to grow, but to build a model that can consistently deliver results and be scaled over time.
