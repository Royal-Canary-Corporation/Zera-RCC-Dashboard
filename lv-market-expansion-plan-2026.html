Player Lifecycle Management
  • Acquisition: Welcome offer, host introduction, first-visit profiling
  • Activation: 2nd visit incentive within 30 days, tier qualification path
  • Retention: Monthly statement, personalized offers, birthday recognition
  • Re-activation: Lapsed player (45+ days) outreach with tailored incentive
  • Upgrade path: Quarterly tier review, upgrade notifications, aspirational messaging
Hotel Conversion — Quick Win

Le Méridien Saigon: ~200–300 rooms, avg 70% occupancy = 140–210 room-nights/night.
If 5% of hotel guests visit LV = +7–10 new players/day.
Avg first-timer ADT $1,500 → $10,500–$15,000 incremental daily turnover.
Target: hotel conversion rate from ~2% → 8% within 6 months.

Expat Segment — Medium Term

HCM has ~250,000 registered foreign residents. Top gaming nationalities: Korean, Chinese, Japanese.
Target 50 active new expat players in Y1 at avg ADT $5,000 = $250,000 ADT base.
Monthly: 2 visits avg = $500,000/month turnover = $6M annualized per 50 players.
BD manager dedicated to expat segment pays back in 3–4 months at $6M/year.

VIP Deepening — Highest ROI

Assume top 20 players currently = 50% of turnover (~$490M/year, $24.5M avg per player).
Increase visit frequency of top tier by 1 visit/month = +15–20% turnover from existing base.
Zero acquisition cost — purely retention/service investment.
1 additional visit/month per Diamond player = $1.5–$2M additional GGR.
