C2 · In-House · 540m² · Floor 2 — Centre Buffer

Premium
Steakhouse & Grill

"Live fire, river light,
the theatre of the cut."

Open kitchen centred on live fire. Long timber bar running the length of the room — doubles as wine bar after 9PM. Industrial steel meets warm timber. River views framed by floor-to-ceiling glass. The cooking is the show. No brand pretension — just product confidence.

C4 · In-House · 520m² · Floor 2 — Opposite End from C1

Indian Fusion
/ ASEAN-Localized

"Warm, aromatic, generous —
the table as communal ritual."

Terracotta and saffron warmth. Sharing plates designed for the table, not the plate. Modern Indian flavours approachable to Vietnamese palates — spiced, not intimidating. Full vegetarian menu as quiet differentiator. Inspired by Rang (Đà Nẵng) — Michelin Bib Gourmet, Vietnam's first contemporary Indian concept. Rich textiles, hand-thrown pottery, generous natural light.

C3A · In-House · ~240m² · Lobby (Barson) — Front

French Bistro
+ Wine Bar

"Not a theme. A feeling —
loose chairs, zinc bar, afternoon light."

The Indochine touch is light: rattan chairs, vintage botanical POSM, a few framed maps of old Saigon on the menu cover. Nothing heavy-handed. The room itself is Paris — zinc bar top, mismatched bistro chairs, handwritten specials on a chalkboard. Natural wine and French-Vietnamese small plates. Street-accessible. Seven days, morning to last glass.

Design language: Paris zinc-bar bistro (Le Bistrot Paul Bert, Café de Flore aesthetic) + very light Indochine accent expressed exclusively through: (1) rattan bistro chairs (originally French colonial import), (2) vintage botanical prints from old Indochina in POSM, (3) menu cover with faded map of 1940s Saigon. No heavy colonial theming. The room reads Paris — the details whisper Saigon.

C3B · In-House · ~120m² · Lobby — Hidden Adjacent

Speakeasy
Cocktail Bar

"Discovery is the point.
You have to find it first."

Concealed entrance from the bistro — bookshelf door or antique phone booth prop. Forty to fifty guests maximum. Dark velvet, hammered copper bar top, amber Edison bulbs. Craft cocktail programme, short menu. The physical embodiment of Le Méridien's "arts and discovery" brand DNA. No signage. No social media hint. Just the regulars who know.

Latest Recipe+ · ADD Reposition · Existing Space · Ground Floor

Vietnamese Focus
+ ASEAN Rotating

"Every month, a different table.
Same room, new Southeast Asia."

Vietnamese as the permanent heart — live phở station, bún bò, bánh mì artisan. Every month, a rotating ASEAN spotlight alongside: Thai aromatics in March, Mekong Delta in April, Indonesian in May. Same room, different story. Market-fresh aesthetic, open live stations, warm natural light. The only hotel in D1 where breakfast takes you somewhere different each month.
