B. Owner Requirements
# Requirement Detail
1 Consolidate under Caly Maximise contracts consolidated under Caly as general contractor — single accountability point to Owner
2 Lump Sum Total Budget Owner sets one fixed total budget. PM and Caly allocate internally. No line-item approval required by Owner except for large direct contracts
3 PM + Caly Autonomy PM and Caly manage internal distribution. May propose additional large contracts directly with Owner (e.g. windows, built-in furniture) when necessary
4 Open Book on Sub-contracts Owner has right to inspect all sub-contract costs under Caly at any time. No savings-sharing mechanism — visibility only
