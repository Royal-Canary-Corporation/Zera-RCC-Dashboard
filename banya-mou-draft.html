3. PART A — CONSTRUCTION & ENGINEERING DOCUMENTATION MANAGEMENT

3.1 Scope Summary

BEMD shall manage and coordinate the preparation of all construction and engineering documentation for the Project, including:

  • Review of Client-Provided Materials (concept, architectural-technological project, interior design) as basis for design development
  • Identification and recommendation of qualified design firms and engineering consultants across all disciplines: Architectural, Structural, HVAC, Water supply/drainage, Electrical & lighting, Low-current systems & BMS, Specialised SPA systems (saunas, hammam, snow rooms, pools, water features)
  • Management and coordination of all engaged design firms through: S1 — Schematic Design (SD) + Design Development (DD) [60 calendar days]; S2 — IFC/Construction Documents (CD) [60 calendar days]; As-Built Documentation
  • Multidisciplinary coordination and clash review
  • Review of bills of quantities, specifications and technical descriptions
  • Preparation of documentation suitable for submission by RCC to Vietnamese authorities
Note: Obtaining Permits remains RCC's responsibility. BEMD does not act as architect, engineer or designer of record under Vietnamese law.

4. PART B — PROJECT MANAGEMENT SERVICES

4.1 Scope Summary

BEMD shall provide project management services from commencement of construction phase until issuance of Certificate of Construction Acceptance (CCA), including:

  • Preparation and maintenance of Project schedule and milestone tracking
  • Identification and recommendation of Project Contractors for RCC's approval and direct engagement
  • Coordination of Project Contractors, suppliers and other third parties
  • Monitoring construction progress; attendance at coordination meetings; chairing technical reviews
  • Maintenance of project document control system
  • Weekly progress reports + monthly executive reports
  • Site visits by BEMD engineering and PM personnel
  • Review of shop drawings, method statements and submittals
  • Notification to RCC of risks, delays, conflicts and issues
  • Support of commissioning, testing and handover; preparation of CCA technical documentation package
Note: CCA application and all interactions with Vietnamese authorities remain RCC's responsibility.
