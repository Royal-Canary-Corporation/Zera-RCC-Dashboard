1. Logic of Selection — Four-Layer Framework

The 12 attributes were NOT invented. They were derived from a triangulation of four independent source layers:

LayerSource# Attributes DerivedWeight in Framework
Layer 1 Forbes Travel Guide 5-Star Standards (900+ criteria) 4 attributes ~33%
Layer 2 Virtuoso Luxe Report 2026 (2,400+ travel advisors surveyed) 4 attributes ~33%
Layer 3 Aman Brand Philosophy (Doronin interviews + Aman publications + Aman Report 2026) 2 attributes ~17%
Layer 4 Experience Economy (Pine & Gilmore) + UHNWI behavior synthesis (Knight Frank Wealth Report, Hurun, Capgemini) 2 attributes ~17%

Why These Four Layers?

No single source captures UHNWI preferences. Forbes evaluates what hotels DELIVER. Virtuoso captures what guests ASK FOR (via advisors). Aman's own philosophy defines what the BRAND promises. Academic frameworks provide theoretical rigor. Triangulating across all four eliminates any single-source bias.

4. Methodology — How Attributes Were Derived

StepProcessOutput
1. Extraction Extract all criteria/trends/drivers from each source document. Forbes = 900+ criteria collapsed into 6 broad themes. Virtuoso = 8 trends collapsed into 5 themes. Aman = brand pillars extracted from 5 interviews/publications. 19 candidate attributes (raw)
2. De-duplication Merge overlapping concepts. Example: Forbes "anticipatory service" + Aman "invisible service" + Pine & Gilmore "staged authenticity" → A3 "Service Intuition" 14 attributes
3. UHNWI Relevance Filter Remove attributes relevant to mass luxury but NOT to UHNWI. Example: "value for money" → removed (UHNWIs don't optimize for price). "location convenience" → removed (UHNWIs seek remote, not convenient). 12 attributes
4. Scoring Amanpulo scored 1-10 vs each attribute by benchmarking against compset properties. Scores cross-referenced with available public data (TripAdvisor, Forbes ratings). 12 scored attributes
5. Weighting Each attribute weighted by guest-tier importance (4 tiers, weighted by % target mix). Critical = weight 4, Important = weight 3, Nice-to-have = weight 1. Weighted priority scores
