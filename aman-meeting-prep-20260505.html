📐 RCC Investment Thesis — Aman Group

RCC operates a repeating investment framework: identify mispricing → build operating platform → attract tier-1 global partners → hold long-term. The Aman Group opportunity represents the next chapter of this thesis — entering a globally recognised ultra-luxury brand at an inflection point, anchoring RCC's Southeast Asia hospitality platform with a brand that commands among the highest ADRs in the world (~USD 2,000–5,000/night), while establishing a preferential pipeline into future Aman developments across the region.
Pillar 01
Brand Moat & Pricing Power
Aman's UHNW clientele and "anti-hotel" positioning command structural pricing premiums that are nearly impossible to replicate. Loyalty is lifestyle-driven, not points-based.
Pillar 02
SEA Pipeline Access
Vietnam and broader SEA remain underpenetrated for Aman. A strategic equity position gives RCC right-of-first-offer on future developments — owning the land/SPV, not just the management fees.
Pillar 03
HoldCo Leverage
Entry at HoldCo level captures consolidated upside across 35+ properties globally. As PIF potentially looks to rebalance, RCC acquires at a negotiated entry vs. IPO pricing.

🏢 1. Corporate Structure & Ownership

  • Where is the HoldCo domiciled? (Singapore, BVI, Cayman?)
  • Who are the current shareholders? Is PIF still majority holder? Any shareholder seeking to exit?
  • Full cap table: shares outstanding, any unexercised options or warrants?
  • Are there drag-along / tag-along provisions that would affect RCC's entry?
  • Any pre-emption rights from existing shareholders that need to be waived?

💰 2. Valuation & Deal Terms

  • What is the current valuation basis? (EBITDA multiple, asset NAV, DCF?)
  • What % stake is being offered to RCC? Controlling or strategic minority?
  • Liquidity event expectation: IPO, trade sale, or long-term hold?
  • Payment structure: all-cash, deferred, or earn-out component?
  • Is there a locked-up period post-closing for RCC's stake?

📊 3. Financials (to verify in VDR)

  • Revenue, EBITDA, Net Income — consolidated, FY2022–2024
  • RevPAR and Occupancy — consolidated across full portfolio?
  • Total debt at HoldCo level? Net Debt / EBITDA ratio?
  • Committed CAPEX for pipeline properties, especially SEA?
  • Any material off-balance-sheet liabilities or contingent obligations?

🌏 4. SEA Strategy & RCC Synergy

  • How many Aman properties are in active pipeline for SEA? Timeline?
  • Will RCC receive Right of First Offer for SEA development opportunities?
  • Co-investment mechanism: at HoldCo level or SPV per property?
  • Is Aman open to a JV or HMA model for Vietnam-specific sites under RCC's land bank?
  • How does Aman currently manage brand exclusivity in markets with existing local partners?

⚖️ 5. Governance & Investor Rights

  • Board representation commensurate with RCC's stake — how many seats?
  • Veto rights on strategic decisions: new markets, brand extension, debt issuance?
  • Information rights: quarterly financials, annual audited accounts, management access?
  • Anti-dilution protection for future capital raises?
  • Reserved matters requiring RCC approval?

🔄 6. Process, VDR & Timeline

  • When can RCC access the VDR? Who is the contact? Scope of materials?
  • Is there an exclusivity period for RCC to negotiate without parallel processes?
  • Expected timeline: LOI → SPA → Close?
  • Who is Aman's legal counsel? What jurisdiction will the SPA be governed by?
  • Is Aman Group engaging other buyers concurrently? Competitive process?
