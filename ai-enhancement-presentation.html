Third Relationship
Skill Training
AI as your coach — same knowledge, every person, their way
③ Skill Training
SOPs in folders nobody reads
The Problem
Institutional knowledge lives in the heads of senior staff — and walks out the door when they leave. Onboarding is inconsistent. Training is a one-off event, not a system.
How Agentic AI Helps
Feed your SOPs into a training agent. Every employee learns from the same source of truth — but in their own format. The agent adapts.
Learning style adaptation
One SOP. Five ways to absorb it.
VisualDiagrams, walkthroughs, annotated screenshots
ReaderSummaries, bullet checklists, structured guides
ChatQ&A back and forth, "quiz me on this"
FlashcardSpaced repetition, daily 5-minute sessions
Simulation"What would you do if..." scenario practice
The agent can't teach something that contradicts your real policy — the source is your actual documentation.
